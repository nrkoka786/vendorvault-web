Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 6, 2025

VendorVault ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Google Workspace Add-on.

1. Data We Access

VendorVault accesses data in your Google Workspace environment only when you explicitly trigger the add-on. We may process:

  • Email Metadata: Sender, Subject, and Date (to identify vendors).
  • Attachments: PDF contracts and invoices (for analysis).
  • Calendar: We require write access to create reminder events.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to extract non-sensitive metadata (dates, costs, vendor names) from your documents. We do not sell your data. Data is processed ephemerally and is not stored on our servers permanently unless you choose to sync it to your own Google Sheet.
